## User

Add NODE: Gospel-Inflected Rock
Super-family: Rock
Active: 1968–present
Peak: 1969–1972, revival 1990–1997, ongoing since 2020

Definition: Rock built on the musical grammar of Black American 
gospel, independent of lyrical religious content. Not Christian 
rock, which is a market and content category, not a sonic one.

Sonic markers:
- Plagal (IV–I) cadences and amen resolutions
- Call-and-response between lead and backing vocals
- Stacked choir or multi-tracked group vocals
- Hammond organ, upright/electric piano, tambourine, handclaps
- Horn sections in soul voicings
- Vamp-and-build form: repeat a figure, add layers, no modulation
- Communal build to catharsis rather than verse-chorus contrast

Inbound edges:
- Black gospel → Gospel-Inflected Rock [idiom transfer, primary]
- Soul / R&B → Gospel-Inflected Rock [idiom transfer]
- Blues rock → Gospel-Inflected Rock [instrumentation]

Outbound edges:
- → Space rock [idiom transfer: Spacemen 3, Spiritualized]
- → Baggy / Madchester [idiom transfer: Primal Scream]
- → Gothic country / post-punk [idiom transfer: Nick Cave]
- → Indie rock, 2020s [idiom transfer: Geese, Black Midi adjacent]

Anchor artists/works:
The Rolling Stones "You Can't Always Get What You Want" (1969)
The Band, Music from Big Pink (1968)
Aretha Franklin / Staple Singers (source, not member)
Dexys Midnight Runners (1980)
Primal Scream, Screamadelica (1991)
Spiritualized, Ladies and Gentlemen We Are Floating in Space (1997)
Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds, The Boatman's Call era
Alabama Shakes, Sound & Color (2015)
Geese, 3D Country (2023)

Do not merge with: Christian rock, CCM, southern rock, soul, 
blues rock.

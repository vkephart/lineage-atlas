Here's where those bands sit on the map now, and what's missing.

**Where they sit now**
- **Sonic Youth**: the first-listed artist on **Noise Rock** (1984), with *Daydream Nation* and "Teen Age Riot" as its picks. Noise Rock feeds Alternative, Shoegaze, Post-Rock, Math Rock and Post-Hardcore. They have no entry in the artist index, though, so searching "Sonic Youth" may not find them. Pixies, Swans and Big Black are missing from the index too.
- **Fontaines D.C.**: tagged **New Post-Punk** (2019) and **Sprechgesang** (2020). Both sit in the Gaze & Post-Punk family, not the Alternative branch.
- **IDLES**: tagged **New Post-Punk** and **Post-Hardcore**.
- **Tropical Fuck Storm**: not on the map at all.

**What's missing**
1. **No Wave** (NYC, around 1978: DNA, Teenage Jesus and the Jerks, Mars, Glenn Branca). This is the direct parent of Sonic Youth, who came out of Branca's guitar ensembles. At the moment the map only links Experimental straight to Noise Rock, so this step is skipped.
2. **The Australian line**. Tropical Fuck Storm don't fit any US or UK lineage. They come from The Drones, which runs back through The Birthday Party, The Scientists and Beasts of Bourbon (sometimes called swamp rock), and further back to The Saints and Radio Birdman. Amyl and the Sniffers come from the same Australian pub-punk root. One node for this, linked to Noise Rock, Post-Punk and Garage Rock, would cover TFS.
3. **The 2000s post-punk revival** (Interpol, Franz Ferdinand, Yeah Yeah Yeahs, The Rapture). Right now these bands are scattered across Indie, Garage and Dance-Punk. There's no revival wave between 1978 Post-Punk and 2019 New Post-Punk.
4. **Splitting New Post-Punk**. It's dated 2019 and lumps two scenes together:
   - The rowdier 2017–18 wave: IDLES, Shame, Fontaines' *Dogrel*. This is closer to punk and post-hardcore.
   - The art-rock Windmill bands: Squid, black midi, BCNR, Dry Cleaning.

   IDLES' *Brutalism* came out in 2017, so the start date is also late.

My suggested additions are the No Wave and Australian nodes, plus artist-index entries for Sonic Youth, Pixies, Swans, Big Black, Tropical Fuck Storm and The Drones. Should I add those? Should I also do the 2000s revival node and the New Post-Punk split?

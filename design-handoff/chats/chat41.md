Not as a genre node. Oxford is a scene, and the map's nodes are built on shared sonic traits. Those bands don't share any:

- **Ride, Swervedriver:** Shoegaze (Swervedriver leaning heavier and more driving)
- **Supergrass:** Britpop
- **Radiohead:** Art rock, later electronic and post-rock
- **Foals:** Math-pop and dance-punk

Madchester works as a node because the Manchester bands had one recognisable sound: baggy breakbeats, jangle guitar and acid house. Oxford never had one, so its "traits" and "why" would end up as a list of everyone's different styles.

Three ways to cover it anyway:

1. **Scene tag:** label artists by city ("Oxford scene") and add a filter that highlights all of them across their different genres. This shows the scattering, which is the interesting part.
2. **Math-Pop node:** the one Oxford sound that really is local. Foals, Youthmovies and This Town Needs Guns grew out of the 2000s Oxford math scene, between math rock and dance-punk.
3. **Britpop node:** the map doesn't have one yet, so Supergrass currently has nowhere to go.
